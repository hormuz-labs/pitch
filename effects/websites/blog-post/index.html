Notes on slow traveland the art of staying

By Elena Marsh · 12 June 2026

We arrived with a list and left with none of it done. That, it turns out, was the point.

The first morning we walked without a map. The bakery on the corner opened at seven; by half past, the street had filled with the smell of it, and we had learned more about the neighbourhood than any guide could have told us.

Stay long enough to be bored

Boredom is where the place starts to reveal itself. The second week, the fishmonger knew our order. The third, he stopped charging us for the lemons.

"A place is not somewhere you go. It's somewhere you let happen to you."

There is a version of travel that is really just consumption with better light. This is the other kind: quieter, slower, and harder to photograph.

We'll go back. Not to see more, but to see the same things again.
