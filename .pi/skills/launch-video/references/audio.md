# Audio — load only what this film needs

Paths are relative to the launch-video skill directory.

| Current task | Read |
|---|---|
| Choose or change a music bed | `references/audio/music.md` |
| Script/record a narrated film before visual authoring, or revise its read | `references/audio/narration.md` |
| Add sound effects | `references/audio/sfx.md` |
| Mix stems or change their levels | `references/audio/mix.md` |

A music-only film needs no narration module. A level edit needs only mixing.
Reuse existing tracks, cues and successful checks; don't reload every reference.
