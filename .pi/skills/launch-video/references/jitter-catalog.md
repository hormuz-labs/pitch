# Jitter template catalog

Every template on jitter.video/templates/all (408, scraped 2026-09-06), each once under its main family with Jitter's own one-line description, its loop length and its other families in brackets. This is the working vocabulary of motion designers today. `effects-vocabulary.md` says which of these moves a launch film owns and how each maps to an engine field or a project type; this file is for `grep` when you want the neighbours of a move ("what else do they do with a counter?").

The length is the template's loop — one move — not a shot. Most are 2–6s: borrow the move, not the tempo.
