# Check and finish

Review source copy before capturing pictures. Can the shot communicate through
its visual and short label? Remove text that merely explains what should be shown.
Check every on-screen number, name and claim against its source.

For a new complete film, run one `pitch motion audit` and one compact
`pitch motion review` (one settled frame per shot, plus `--times 0`: frame one
must be a finished composition). Read each sheet once, looking for: text over a
busy picture without a backing (aim for 4.5:1 contrast on essential copy), text
colliding with other text, the same layout repeated in neighbouring shots, and
whether the pictures alone, muted, say what changed.
Compare the important motion choices with the selected references: did the
adaptation preserve the mechanism, or collapse into repetitive entrances?
Settled frames cannot answer this. For a signature transition or handoff whose
execution is still unverified, sample its changing state with `--times` and
check staging, continuity and the landing. Do not infer motion quality from a
successful compile or a lab citation.
Batch actual fixes, then re-check affected shots only. Extra `--times` are for
a specific unresolved transition, not a frame-by-frame inspection ritual.
Audit pacing notes are answered in `references/pacing.md`. A contact sheet
does not prove normal-speed playback or perfect motion.

Finish music/SFX and mixing using the relevant modules in `references/audio.md`;
narration was recorded before visual authoring, not written here to fit the cut.
The preview and Export use `audio/mix.wav` automatically. After successful checks,
report actual runtime and any unresolved issue, separating what you checked
from what still needs a person to watch or listen to, then stop. The user owns launch
MP4 export; do not render a review copy or run a routine paid film review.

For edits, preserve timing for cosmetic changes. Retiming an action means
updating dependent cues/sound. Reuse successful checks unless relevant inputs
changed; audio-only edits need no visual audit. Existing-media analysis is
available for a specific question, not a reason to produce another video.
