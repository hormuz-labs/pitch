# Teaser

Build anticipation around one reveal. Decide what to withhold, what gives the
viewer a reason to care, and what they should remember at the end. A partial
object, a product gesture or a brief phrase can carry the setup.

Let the reveal land, then end on the useful next step, mark or date. A teaser
does not need a feature tour or an arbitrary sub-20-second limit. Use the least
copy and shortest complete sequence that communicates the idea.
