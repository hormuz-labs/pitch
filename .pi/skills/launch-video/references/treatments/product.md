# Composed product walkthrough

Show one meaningful task from input to visible result. Use real screens or
faithful, simplified product details. Crop or focus the relevant control rather
than reproducing an entire interface at tiny scale. Use recognizable integration
icons to establish context and a short label to identify an action.

Let the action demonstrate the claim; avoid a paragraph describing what the
viewer should be seeing. Hold on the result before moving on. If the brief asks
to actually drive and record a browser session, use the separate demo-video
skill instead of fabricating that recording as motion graphics.
