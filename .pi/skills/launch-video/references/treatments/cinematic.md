# Cinematic launch

Choose one product-specific visual idea and an emotional progression. Compose
strong frames using scale, light, materials and deliberate negative space.
Alternate anticipation, movement and settled payoff instead of keeping every
element moving. A simple transformation can connect several environments or
stages of a task without a sequence of explanatory cards.

Use product imagery, objects or sourced footage according to the concept.
Generated footage belongs only where no real product source is needed; consult
the separate generated-video skill if using it. Cinematic does not imply 3D,
narration, a slow camera, a dark ground or a fixed shot count. Sound follows the brief.
