# Feature announcement

Make one useful change visible. Show the relevant before/action/after, with
the feature's result as the payoff. A message becoming a resolved task is more
direct than a card claiming operational efficiency.

Use the actual product behavior and recognizable source/destination icons.
Keep supporting features out unless they are necessary to explain this change.
Choose a concise label and let motion demonstrate the rest; finish with a clear
next step rather than another summary of everything just shown.
