# Reference studies

Five studied ads, beat by beat, to consult when a brief resembles one of
them. They show what works, not what to copy: never reuse their footage,
words, claims, music or brand. The shared grammar is in
[social-ad.md](social-ad.md); read that first.

| Ad | Look | Length | Voice | Cuts | Opening 5s | Master |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A — hearing protection | editorial DTC | 49s | 2.75 w/s | 2.1/s overall, bursts of 0.08s | "Here's the thing", a burst of faces under "1.1 BILLION" | −13.7 LUFS |
| B — men's acne device | editorial DTC | 95s | 3.25 w/s, one pause in 90s | 0.64/s overall, bursts of 0.1s | 11 cuts: "Here's the thing", articles, faces under "OVER THE AGE [25]" | −11.2 LUFS |
| C — B2B sales software | cinematic B2B | 33s | 3.68 w/s | 1.19/s | speed-ramped money, watch, phones under "LOSING… UNREACHABLE…" | −9.1 LUFS |
| D — women's serum | loud DTC | 76s | 2.77 w/s | 1.20/s, 15 cuts in the first 5s | "Mothers +40", seven faces in 0.7s under "YOUNG Enough", "KEEP WATCHING" | −22.4 LUFS* |
| E — men's supplement | loud DTC | 68s | 3.07 w/s | 1.06/s | "This is not an apple — it's a $60 million piece of biotech" | −23.5 LUFS* |

*D and E were delivered quiet; platforms normalise loudness, so match their
dynamics, not their level.

## A — hearing protection (editorial DTC, 49s)

| Time | Voice (paraphrased) | Picture | Captions | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–0.7 | — | two flash frames of the world at its best (festival crowd, confetti) | — | the stakes before the problem |
| 0.7–1.6 | "Here's the thing" | black | HERE'S / THE / THING | the hook: someone is about to tell you something |
| 1.6–4.0 | "1.1 billion people…" | B&W portraits, a new face every 0.12–0.3s | giant "1" → "1." → "1.1", then BILLION | the stake at human scale |
| 4.0–5.2 | "…losing their hearing" | a chromatic sine wave on black | LOSING / THEIR / HEARING | the subject as one image |
| 5.2–7.7 | "because 15 minutes at 85 dB…" | two source pages flickering, the figure highlighted | BECAUSE (small) | proof, cited |
| 7.7–11.3 | "…damage your hearing forever" | hourglass; a blast; faces in pain; FOREVER | FOREVER | consequence, made literal |
| 11.3–14.4 | "like staring into the sun without sunglasses" | an eye in a blade of light; a hand against the sun | — | an analogy everyone feels |
| 14.4–17.3 | "we're in a hearing health pandemic" | glitch → the wave again → the Earth | WE'RE / IN / A / HEARING / HEALTH, PANDEMIC | reframe: a problem the size of the world |
| 17.3–22.5 | "and we know, earplugs suck. They make music muffled…" | glitch; black cards; club silhouettes | EARPLUGS SUCK (big, glitching), MUFFLED (blurred) | the viewer's objection, said for them |
| 22.5–26.0 | "that is why we decided to make one…" | CAD on a laptop, a factory, sketches, prototypes — 0.4s each | — | the turn: real people made this |
| 26.0–27.2 | (a breath) | product renders turning | — | silence before the name |
| 27.2–33.3 | "This is [brand]… membrane filters… a second pair of eardrums" | product in the air, its case, an exploded view, the filter with vibration passing into it | — | reveal, then mechanism |
| 33.5–38.0 | "…effective protection…" | the product in hand; people laughing in bars and concerts | — | proof of life: the promise delivered |
| 38.2–43.3 | "Thank us when you finally experience what clear music sounds like" | the product floating on white | THANK / US / WHEN / YOU → FINALLY → EXPERIENCE → WHAT / CLEAR / MUSIC / SOUNDS / LIKE | payoff: the feeling, not the spec |
| 43.5–49.0 | — | logo on black with drifting particles; social handle | — | brand, with room |

Sound: the mix performs the problem — the bed ducks ~10dB under the "muffled"
stretch (4.8–13.5s) and slams back +15–19dB on the pandemic reframe; 0.45s of
dead silence at 19.0s detonates +35dB into EARPLUGS SUCK — the film's loudest
moment, on the objection, not the turn (the turn and the product reveal then
*lift*, +12–17dB). A breath before the name, another 0.5s before the logo.
−13.7 LUFS, LRA 8.0: the hottest and most dynamic master of the five.

## B — men's acne device (editorial DTC, 95s)

Black-and-teal problem act, footage in small glowing rounded windows, mixed
weights inside one caption line, hand-drawn arrows on product shots; the
whole solution act flips to white.

| Time | Beat | Picture and type |
|---|---|---|
| 0–1.5 | cold open, hook | a sickly teal face; HERE'S / THE / THING on black |
| 1.5–3.8 | stake burst | article pages flicker; faces every 0.1–0.2s under a steady OVER THE AGE [25] |
| 3.8–8 | the problem | STILL SUFFER FROM ACNE on scratched black; selfies framed as phone windows "feel like little boys" |
| 8–18 | cause | article on testosterone; EXCESSIVE OIL PRODUCTION over orange smoke; "can last FOREVER." underlined; a flaming sword for "double-edged sword" |
| 18–30 | the enemy | "But **the skincare industry** doesn't make it easy…" mixed weights over a pharmacy aisle; bottles with drawn arrows "made for women [or]…"; the drug as a white card |
| 30–40 | personal | "I was one of those guys"; a list, one card each: SHADOW OF MYSELF / LOWER CONFIDENCE / FEWER DATES / LESS MOTIVATION |
| 40–48 | social proof of the pain | OVER TWO-THIRDS OF PEOPLE ADMIT THEY FIND ACNE UNATTRACTIVE over a crowd |
| 48.5–54 | the turn | I GOT TIRED OF IT. over a screaming face — then **white**: I DECIDED TO TAKE MATTERS INTO MY OWN HANDS. The music peaks here |
| 54.5–60 | reveal | FLEX giant beside the product on white; THE FIRST SKINCARE TOOL BUILT TO TARGET THE ROOT CAUSE OF ACNE |
| 60–72 | mechanism in use | creators using it: NO HARSH CHEMICALS, NO QUICK FIXES; macro "sonic tech"; water on skin DEEP CLEANS YOUR PORES |
| 72–85 | proof stack | the kit glowing with callouts; "has already TRANSFORMED over 10, → 50,000 men" over before/after pairs; [BREAKOUTS] [FRUSTRATION] tags; a wall of reviews |
| 85–94 | payoff | "Transform into a new man today", "be treated like the king you are"; YOU DESERVE IT. repeating as it dissolves |

Sound: bass thinned under the "industry" stretch (18–30s), full again and at
its peak on the turn ("I got tired… so I decided"); a voice with almost no
pauses; numbers read slower ("forty percent", "fifty thousand").

## C — B2B sales software (cinematic B2B, 33s)

Archival and vintage footage as metaphor (a trading floor, rotary phones,
pocket watches, stacks of cash), speed-ramped into the first words; the
product's chat UI small and centred on black; a serif wordmark; a URL typed
into a search bar as the close.

| Time | Beat | Picture and type |
|---|---|---|
| 0–3.8 | the loss | a light tunnel, a bill, a pocket watch, a finger on a page: LOSING / BEST SALES OPPORT… / AMONG / UNREACHABLE PROSPE… — a word per image |
| 4–7.9 | the cost | a phone hung up: SO YOU'RE LEAVING; cash on a table: ON THE TABLE.; a "$7" bill slams; ENDS TODAY. on a gradient card |
| 8–12 | the question, the answer | the product's chat UI on black types the viewer's question; "We've got your back." |
| 12–15 | reveal | the serif wordmark alone; "Meet your new sales team" over a framed 1970s sales floor; a rising chart |
| 15–26 | how it works | an AI wireframe hand; a ring of fire "prospecting software"; a smiling user; "They make the call, set the appointment, serve it on a silver platter" — a handwritten note, a framed handshake; a dial [24/7] |
| 26–33 | close | wordmark + URL typed into a search bar; "Welcome to the digital age gold rush"; DON'T [miss out]; "Book a call today" |

Sound: a driving 91 BPM bed under a fast, clipped voice (3.68 w/s);
sentence-final words land long ("table.", "today.", "Belfort."), and a surge
lands on them every 4–6s (measured at 4.3, 8.3, 14.3s) — the bed keeps the
time the cuts don't.

## D — women's serum (loud DTC, 76s)

Neon lime as the brand's structural colour (flood cards, frame borders,
highlights); condensed italic display type mixed with serif italics; a small
running subtitle under every big word; emoji stickers; an offer close.

| Time | Beat | Picture and type |
|---|---|---|
| 0–4 | call-out hook | "Mothers +40"; "If *you* WANT to *look*" (mixed faces); seven faces in 0.7s under YOUNG Enough, the last shrinking into a lime-bordered window; DAUGHTER / MOM labels on pairs |
| 4.5–5.5 | retention hook | KEEP WATCHING in chromatic type on black |
| 6–12 | the claim | ONE *drop* of — a dropper on white; 3000% STRONGER over a highlighted article line; faces: GROW YOUR new skin |
| 12–26 | what you'd no longer need | HERE'S TRUTH; YOU WOULDN'T NEED over retinol, needles, filler; sick-face emoji stickers for "silly nonsense"; ZERO PROBLEMS TO FIX; cash for "money in your pocket" |
| 26–31 | anaphora | NO — sun damage, NO — dark spots, NO — sagging: one close-up of skin per NO, on the beat |
| 31–42 | enemy and price anchor | rich women; IT'S NOT knocked out over fish ("not fish bones"); a microscope "how POWERFUL"; "$20,000 per treatment" over a woman |
| 42.5–57 | reveal and proof | an apple and stem-cell stickers; an article highlighted in lime; FRACTION OF THE NORMAL STEM CELLS on a dollar bill; a "100% ethical" stamp |
| 57.5–76 | offer close | "beauty time machine"; NO BRAINER; SAVE MONEY AND SAVE FACE; TRY IT RISK FREE; 365 DAYS; YEAR GUARANTEE; AND EXPERIENCE FOR YOURSELF |

Sound: the bass stays out under the hook and comes in hard on the claim
("3,000%"); a second breakdown and return around the list; the voice warm and
conversational, slowing on every number.

## E — men's supplement (loud DTC, 68s)

A whisper/shout rhythm: a tiny italic word alone on white, then a full-bleed
image with one huge word in a clashing face (blackletter, neon, blurred red);
objects as jokes (a WD-40 can, a Swiss Army knife, a receipt); a web page
scrolled as the close.

| Time | Beat | Picture and type |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8.5 | pattern-interrupt hook | an apple in a lime frame: "This is not an apple"; "$60" counting to $60,000,000; a figure dissolving into particles: SHATTER; paper pieces for "a billion little pieces" |
| 9–15 | the grievance | tiny "When was the last time" on white; blackletter *good*; MADE FOR MEN; "I can't name anything since" — a WD-40 can |
| 15–28 | the enemy | athletes in lime frames "looking 28, feeling 23"; TAKEN ~~seriously~~; glass towers "corporations"; *Fruity* skin creams, TRT, pills; WRONG in red glow; "we're not fruits, we're MEN" |
| 28–44 | reveal and claims | an article on apple stem cells; a Swiss Army knife "of men's youth"; a lab; ×34 → ×300 over an MRI; NO BRAINER |
| 45–59 | benefits | "+35 dudes" counting; MENTALLY / PHYSICALLY; More *libido*, better *joint* health, sharper *brain*; a receipt "the list is long, unlike your…" |
| 59–68 | close | the apple with a hand-drawn note; the URL in a pill with a cursor clicking; the landing page scrolling |

Sound: tight 115 BPM bed with a small surge every few seconds on the
section's key word; one pause in 67 seconds.
